**Stakeholder** is any person, group or org that influences, affected by program, provide resources or support , has decision making authority
Stakeholders' management process involves stakeholders remain informed, engaged, committed throughout the program lifecycle

**Right stakeholders + right engagement + right communication = Program success**

Key Stakeholders Group
1. Program Sponsor: Champions the program, Secures organization support, ultimately accountable for success of program.
2. Steering committee: Strategic guidance, Decision-making support, escalation resolution like Chief Operations officer, CFO, Representative, IT representative
3. Portfolio Manager: Ensures program aligns with business strategy
4. Management Stakeholders: Program manager, Project Manager, PMO(Program management office),
5. Implementation and operations stakeholders: Program management team, Project teams, Suppliers/Vendors
6. Financial & Regulatory stakeholders

   Stakeholder management process
   1. Validate scope: Strategic alignment exists, roadmap is clear, Benefits are measureable, Scope is clearly defined.
   2. Identify Stakeholders: Who influences success? Who provides resources? who approves decisions? Who is impacted
   3. Create Stakeholder register: Name, Role, Responsibilities, Expectations, Influence, Impact, Engagement timeline
   4. Stakeholder analysis: Influence, Interest, Impact, Commitment
   5. Engagement planning: When, How to engage, what information to provide
   6. Communication planning: What, who, when, how
